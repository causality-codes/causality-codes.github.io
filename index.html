Kramers–Kronig transformation

We collect various codes to compute the Kramers–Kronig transformation and process optical spectroscopy data.

python 3.12+ numpy · scipy pytest

The Kramers–Kronig relations connect the real and imaginary parts of any causal linear response function.

$$\chi_1(\omega) = \frac{1}{\pi} \mathcal{P}\!\!\int_{-\infty}^\infty \frac{\chi_2(\omega')}{\omega' - \omega}\,d\omega'$$

The principal value makes this awkward to evaluate directly. Every method here sidesteps the singularity by expanding \(\varepsilon_2\) in a basis whose transform is known in closed form — so the numerics reduce to a matrix product.
