Kramers–Kronig transformation

We collect various codes to compute the Kramers–Kronig transformation and process optical spectroscopy data.

Kramers–Kronig relations connect the real and imaginary parts of any causal linear response function.

$$\chi_1(\omega) = \frac{1}{\pi} \mathcal{P}\!\!\int_{-\infty}^\infty \frac{\chi_2(\omega')}{\omega' - \omega}\,d\omega'$$

You probably know this already if you are here. So let's get to business. Below you will find tools to help you process and make sense of your data.
